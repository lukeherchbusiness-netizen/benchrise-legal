BENCHRISE

Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the BenchRise app ("BenchRise", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions? Email benchrisesupport@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your device

Your plan answers (such as bodyweight, bench max, goal, and schedule), workout log, food log, daily macro targets, and unit preference are stored on your phone. We do not receive this information unless you choose to post it, for example by adding a lift to the leaderboard.

Information you give us when you create an account

  • Email address and password. Used to sign you in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us.
  • Username and profile picture. These are public. Anyone using the leaderboard can see them.
  • Lifts you post. The weight, reps, division (raw or wraps), date, and the photo or video link you provide as proof. These are public on the leaderboard.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report content or block a user, we store that so we can review reports and hide blocked users from you.

Please do not put personal information such as your address or phone number in your username, photos, or videos.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Apple. Apple processes your payment, and we never see your card number or Apple ID. We use RevenueCat, a subscription service, to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase details such as which subscription you hold and when it renews.

Camera and photos

  • Camera. Used only to scan food barcodes. Images are not saved or uploaded. Only the barcode number is sent to look up the food.
  • Photos. Used only if you choose a profile picture or lift proof. Only the photos you select are uploaded.

Food search

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search words or barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a public food database, to get nutrition information. We do not send your name or account details with those requests.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores account information, profiles, lifts, uploaded photos and videos, reports, and blocks.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status.
  • Apple handles payments and distributes the app.
  • Open Food Facts provides food data.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking.

How long we keep information

We keep your account information and posts until you delete them or your account. Deleted accounts are removed along with your profile, lifts, and uploaded files.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile or delete any lift you posted in the app.
  • Delete your account. In the app, go to the Me tab and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, lifts, and uploaded files.
  • You can cancel a subscription anytime in your iPhone Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.
  • You can email us to ask what we hold about you or to request deletion.

Children

BenchRise is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it. Users under 18 should train with a coach or experienced adult and get a parent or guardian's permission before using the app.

Security

We use reputable providers and encrypted connections, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
